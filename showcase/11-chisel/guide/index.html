How CHISEL was carved

Written by Claude Fable 5, autonomously. This is the deepest pipeline in the showcase: a sentence became a photograph, the photograph became geometry, and the geometry became something you can walk around and light with your hand. Total asset cost: about twenty credits and four minutes.

1 · The three translations

  1. Sentence → image. Higgsfield soul_2: "classical veiled-marble bust, thin stone as translucent fabric, black room, one key light." The veiled-marble tradition (Corradini, Strazza) was chosen deliberately — drapery is the hardest thing to fake and the most convincing thing to mesh.
  2. Image → mesh. The image job ID went straight into image_to_3d — untextured on purpose. Baked textures carry baked light; raw geometry lets the browser own the lighting, which is the whole show. Result: a 0.92MB GLB, 30,986 triangles, with a finished back the source photo never showed.
  3. Mesh → marble. GLTFLoader, every mesh re-skinned with one MeshStandardMaterial (near-white, roughness .5), normalized via Box3 to sit on the plinth. ACES tone mapping + soft shadow maps.

2 · The cursor is the key light

angle  = -mouseX * 2.6 + cameraTheta   // swing around the piece
height =  1.2 - mouseY * 2.2            // raise / lower
key.position.set(3·sin(angle), height, 3·cos(angle))

One warm SpotLight with real shadows, riding a dome mapped to the pointer, against a faint cool rim. Chiaroscuro as an input device — the visitor performs the lighting the original photograph could only freeze.

3 · Honesty as luxury

  • The placard's dimensions and triangle count are computed from the loaded mesh, not typed in.
  • The GLB is downloadable. A museum that lets you take the statue home.
  • The process section admits the pipeline outright — in 2026, the pipeline is the provenance.

4 · The three passes

  1. Verified the mesh loads, centers, and the light rig responds (two screenshots, opposite shadows).
  2. Killed a blue cast on the plinth (ambient desaturation, rim −30%), calmed veil speckle with rougher marble.
  3. Verified the live placard data, loading veil, failure copy, and mobile camera pullback.

5 · Replicate it

  • Generate untextured meshes when you plan to light them yourself.
  • Pick subjects whose difficulty is geometric (drapery, folds) — that's where image-to-3D impresses.
  • Give the visitor one physical verb. Here it's light.
  • Deploy: netlify deploy --prod --dir .
