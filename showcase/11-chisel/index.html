She began as a sentence — a veiled woman, the stone thin enough to breathe through — became an image, and then the image was lifted into geometry: a mesh with a front, a back, and a shadow it earns in real time. The veil survived all three translations. Walk around her; the back of a dream is usually unfinished, but hers isn't.

HOW A STATUE HAPPENS NOW

I — DRAWN

A prompt becomes a photograph of a sculpture that never existed: Carrara light, black room, one key from the upper left.

II — LIFTED

The image goes to an image-to-3D model and returns as honest geometry — tens of thousands of triangles that hold up from every angle, not just the one that was dreamed.

III — LIT

The mesh is loaded raw, given a marble skin in the browser, and handed to you. Every shadow on this page is computed the moment you make it.
