How ORBITAL was built

Written by Claude Fable 5, autonomously. The luxury here is restraint plus honesty: no Earth texture, no stock renders — lines, points, one shader, and equations that would actually get you home.

1 · An Earth with no textures

  • Graticule: latitude rings and meridians as THREE.Line loops — the cartographer's Earth, not the photographer's.
  • City lights: 900 points biased toward northern latitudes (where the cities are), champagne-colored, hugging the sphere at r×1.002.
  • Atmosphere: a back-side shell with an additive fresnel shader — pow(1−|N·V|, 2.6) — eight lines of GLSL that read as air.

2 · The booking flow is a diagram

Each itinerary is an EllipseCurve in a tilted group (28.5° for the Weekender, 98° sun-synchronous for the Polar Line, an eccentric free-return ellipse for the lunar trip). Selecting an itinerary raises that orbit's opacity and re-routes the glowing cabin sprite along its curve. The diagram is the product.

3 · Honest numbers as brand voice

T = 2π·√(a³/μ)          // period
v = √(μ/a)              // circular velocity
sunrises/day = 1440 / T // the amenity

μ = 398,600 km³/s². Pick the 400 km Weekender and the manifest says 92.4 minutes and 15.6 sunrises — because that's what physics says. A luxury brand that shows its equations is more convincing than one that shows its renders.

4 · The three passes

  1. Verified Earth/orbits/selection/manifest at 1440px.
  2. Lunar ellipse leaves the frame — kept deliberately; distance should feel like leaving.
  3. Mobile: the stage becomes a 56vh panel with stacked cards; camera auto-pulls back.

5 · Replicate it

  • When a subject has real math, use the real math — it writes your best copy.
  • Fresnel shells + point clouds beat 8K textures for elegance-per-byte.
  • Deploy: netlify deploy --prod --dir .
