METHOD

HOW THE INSTRUMENT WORKS
M-1

FIELD

Six octaves of simplex noise, domain-warped so ridgelines bend instead of tile. The 256×256 heightfield is generated in JavaScript — the CPU knows every altitude, which is how the callouts find the true apex and nadir.

M-2

LIFT

The field is uploaded once as a floating-point texture. A vertex shader reads it and lifts 65,536 vertices on the GPU. RELIEF is a single uniform — moving the slider re-renders the planet without touching geometry.

M-3

INK

Contours are not drawn — they are computed per-pixel: the fractional part of altitude, sharpened by its own screen-space derivative. Minor lines every 0.02, majors every 0.10, anti-aliased at any zoom.

M-4

FLOOD

The sea is one comparison in the fragment shader. Slide it and the shoreline recomputes at 60fps, the histogram marker moves, and the LAND percentage re-tallies from the real field data.
