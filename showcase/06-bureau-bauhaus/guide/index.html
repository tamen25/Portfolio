How the Bureau was built

Written by Claude Fable 5, autonomously. The thesis: don't make a page about the Bauhaus — make the workshop. The visitor leaves with a poster they composed.

1 · The printing table

  • Shapes are plain data — {type, x, y, size, rotation, colorIndex} — rendered as absolutely-positioned divs (circle, square, bar, triangle via clip-path, arc via two-sided border-radius, halftone via radial-gradient background tiling).
  • Pointer events drag; a sub-5px pointerup counts as a click and cycles the color; double-click rotates 15°. Every shape is focusable: arrows move, C recolors, R rotates.
  • PNG export: the same shape data renders to an offscreen 1200×1800 canvas — each DOM primitive has a canvas twin (arc becomes ctx.arc stroke, halftone becomes a dot loop). document.fonts.load ensures Jost is ready before the caption is drawn, then toDataURL + a click on a temp anchor.
  • Shuffle guarantees the primary triad — random palettes kept dealing all-black compositions (pass 2 fix).

2 · Doctrine as interaction

Kandinsky's 1923 questionnaire (triangle=yellow, square=red, circle=blue) becomes three cards whose shapes flip 180° on hover — the theory is the hover state.

3 · Discipline

  • One typeface (Jost, the Futura descendant), five colors, 4px black rules everywhere.
  • No images, no libraries — the whole site is HTML/CSS + ~230 lines of vanilla JS.
  • Timeline ends with the honest 2026 entry: "You are dragging rectangles on a website because of them."

4 · The three passes

  1. Verified hero collage, composer, marquee, cards, timeline at 1440px.
  2. Fixed all-dark shuffles (triad guarantee); exercised the download path — zero errors.
  3. Mobile restack at 390px; keyboard + reduced-motion checks.
