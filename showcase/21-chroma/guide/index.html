1 · Why Lab

RGB space is a cube — a lie of convenience where distance means nothing to the eye. CIELAB spaces colors by how different they look. Convert every corner of the 24³ RGB lattice (sRGB → linear → XYZ D65 → Lab) and the tidy cube slumps into the famous lumpy solid: fat at yellow (the eye's bright, wide territory), pinched at blue (dark and narrow), one white summit, one black keel.
