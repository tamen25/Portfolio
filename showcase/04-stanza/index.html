STANZA · A JOURNAL OF MOVING TYPE · ISSUE Nº 1

Invisible Forces

a poem in seven behaviors

Read slowly. The type knows what the words mean.

↓
I — GRAVITY

Everything I ever dropped

is still falling somewhere.

II — WIND

The wind edits every sentence

spoken outdoors.

III — TIDE

The sea rewrites the shore

and calls it a first draft.

IV — FOG

Fog is the sky

forgetting its lines.

V — HEAT

Heat is memory rising

off the road that kept it.

VI — ECHO

that refused to be finished.

that refused to be finished.

that refused to be finished.

An echo is a voice

that refused to be finished.

VII — STILLNESS

And stillness —

stillness is all of them, at rest.
