HOW THE DECK WORKS

Written by Claude Fable 5, autonomously. SIDE B has no audio files. Every note is synthesized the moment you press play — which means every play really is a slightly different take, like a proper dub.

1 · A LO-FI ENGINE IN 120 LINES

  • Clock: the classic Web Audio lookahead scheduler — a 25ms setInterval schedules everything inside a 120ms horizon against AudioContext.currentTime. Sample-accurate, tab-jank-proof.
  • Kick: a sine that pitch-drops 140→42Hz in 110ms. Hat: a 60ms noise burst through a 7kHz highpass. Bass: triangle notes off a 16-step pattern. Pad: two detuned saws per chord note, slow attack, closing lowpass.
  • Tape hiss: a looped noise buffer through a highpass, mixed at 1–2% — the cheapest authenticity money can't buy.
  • Each shelf tape is just a parameter set: BPM, root note, chord loop, bass pattern, filter cutoff, hiss level. Hot-swapping restarts the scheduler on the same context.

2 · PLASTIC WITHOUT PHOTOS

  • The deck and cassette are pure CSS: layered gradients for plastic, inset shadows for mold lines, radial-gradient screws with a rotated slot pseudo-element.
  • Reels are three-spoke divs on a spin animation whose animation-play-state is gated by a .playing class — REW/FF simply flip direction and shorten the duration while held.
  • The VU meters read a real AnalyserNode RMS every frame; the right channel gets a little jitter because perfect stereo symmetry reads as fake.

3 · THE COPY IS THE MERCHANDISE

Tracklists like "the dial tone, slowed 800%" and "fridge hum in F minor" do the world-building. A fake shop is only as good as its inventory.

4 · THE THREE PASSES

  1. Transport + hot-swap verified (label rewrites, counter resets, reels gate correctly).
  2. Fixed a CSS custom-property inheritance bug — track numbers showed the wrong tape color because --tp lived on an inner span instead of the button.
  3. Mobile restack + reduced-motion (reels pause; sound remains user-initiated).

5 · REPLICATE IT

  • Synthesize, don't stream: a 4-voice loop costs zero bytes and never gets a DMCA.
  • Gate your animations on state classes so the UI can't lie about what's playing.
  • Deploy: netlify deploy --prod --dir .
