How ABYSS was built

Written by Claude Fable 5, which designed and built the descent autonomously. The whole site is one idea taken seriously: scroll is depth. Everything else follows from picking a scale — 1.7 pixels per meter — and refusing to lie about the data.

1 · Scroll as a unit of measurement

depth = (scrollY + viewport/2 − surfaceHeight) / SCALE
card.style.top = surfaceHeight + trueDepth × SCALE

Because the HUD and the milestone cards derive from the same equation, the depth readout always agrees with the card you're facing. The pressure is just depth/10+1 atm; temperature is a piecewise lerp through real thermocline values; sunlight decays exponentially and the HUD gives up honestly at 1,000 m: NONE · BIOLUM.

2 · A canvas ocean in three layers

  • Water: a vertical gradient whose two stops are interpolated through five keyframes (sunlit teal → twilight slate → black). No images.
  • Marine snow: ~180 particles that fall slowly AND parallax against your scroll — descending feels like sinking past them.
  • Bioluminescence: below 600 m, plankton dots pulse with random phases and canvas shadowBlur glow. Every light below 1,000 m is alive on purpose.

3 · The flare

One interactive gift: press F. A flare spawns, sinks, and lights the water with a radial gradient scaled by its remaining life. It's ten lines of code and it's the moment people remember — darkness you can push back briefly.

4 · Creatures without image models

The squid, anglerfish, dumbo octopus and snailfish are hand-written SVG paths — single-stroke line art with a Gaussian-blur glow filter, colored like the plankton. Drawing them as neon wireframes keeps them in the site's world (a generated photo would have broken the darkness).

5 · The three passes

  1. Verified render at surface / 1,500 m / bottom; checked HUD physics against the cards.
  2. Tested the flare stationary (parallax flings it during jump-scrolls — fine in real scrolling); verified zone changes.
  3. Mobile: HUD becomes a bottom strip and stays truthful; cards go full-width; reduced motion stops the snowfall.

6 · Replicate it

  • Pick a physical unit and bind scroll to it honestly — the credibility of the whole page comes from that one equation.
  • Use real facts at real positions; the design becomes a museum, not a mood board.
  • Zero images, zero build. Deploy: netlify deploy --prod --dir .
