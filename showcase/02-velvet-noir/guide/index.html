How this site was made

Written by Claude Fable 5, which designed and built VELVET NOIR autonomously. This is the flagship of the generated-asset pipeline: every image and the hero film were generated, curated, edited and art-directed by the model.

1 · The asset pipeline

  1. Mood sweep. A headless browser searched Pinterest for dark luxury perfume editorial campaign. Four references confirmed the world: smoke silhouettes, materials on dark stone, candlelight against black velvet.
  2. Generation. Five images with Higgsfield soul_2 (editorial/fashion model): a hero still, a liquid macro, a veiled muse with a taper candle, a Dutch-master materials flat-lay, and a three-flacon lineup. One consistent prompt vocabulary — candlelit chiaroscuro — keeps the set cohesive.
  3. The film. The hero still was fed to kling3_0_turbo as a start frame with a cinemagraph prompt: smoke curls, flame flickers, slow push-in, nothing else moves. Five seconds, loops as the site's living hero. 7.5 credits.
  4. Optimization. Everything converted to WebP (≤140KB each); the film is a 3.3MB MP4 with the still as poster, muted, looped, paused under prefers-reduced-motion.

2 · The label problem (a lesson)

Image models love writing gibberish on perfume bottles. The first lineup said “MME PEPIRRME CALDHERON.” A re-roll with aggressive no-text prompting still whispered “SERE” on a label. The fix was an edit pass: the image went through nano_banana_pro with the instruction to replace labels with plain matte glass while keeping lighting identical. Lesson: don't re-roll what you can surgically edit — and always zoom into generated imagery before shipping it.

3 · Design system

  • Type: Bodoni Moda — the couture didone — for display and italic accents; Inter Tight for body and letterspaced caps labels. No mono font: this house doesn't own a terminal.
  • Palette: noir #070507, velvet #100910, candle amber #E2A45B, bone #EFE7DB, gold hairlines at 10–22%.
  • Signature: La Pyramide — the olfactory pyramid as an interactive diagram. Tabs switch the eau; strata answer on hover. Structure is information: the pyramid is how perfumers actually describe composition.
  • Motion: GSAP + ScrollTrigger with SRI-pinned CDN scripts. One entrance timeline for the hero, scroll reveals per band, a sheen sweep across product imagery on hover, slow parallax on the film.

4 · The three iteration passes

Pass 1 — Verify the choreography

Full-page headless screenshots showed blank sections — a capture artifact (ScrollTriggers don't fire beyond the viewport). Re-QA'd with scrolled viewport shots; every reveal works in real scrolling. Learned to never judge below-fold GSAP work from a full-page capture.

Pass 2 — Asset hygiene

Zoomed into every generated image at 100%. Caught the gibberish labels, ran the edit pass, re-exported. Wired each eau's “Read its pyramid” link to preselect its tab.

Pass 3 — Small screens

A hidden <br> swallowed its space and printed “candleand”; the pyramid's apex clipped its own label at 390px. Both fixed with one-line changes — the kind of bugs only screenshots catch.

5 · Replicate it

  • Generate a consistent world, not individual pictures: one lighting vocabulary across every prompt.
  • Animate a still you already love (image → video start-frame), rather than prompting video from scratch.
  • Edit passes beat re-rolls for surgical fixes.
  • Ship WebP + poster-backed MP4; respect reduced motion.
  • Deploy: netlify deploy --prod --dir .
