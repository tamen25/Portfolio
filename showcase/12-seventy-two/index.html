JUL 7 — JUL 11

小暑 Shōsho · “Lesser heat” — the year leans into summer.

THE FULL YEAR · SCROLL SIDEWAYS · TAP ANY SEASON TO VISIT IT

The seventy-two kō are weather written as poetry: east wind melts the ice; fish emerge from it; the first rainbow of the year. Nothing in them is abstract — each names one thing you could, in principle, step outside and check. The calendar is roughly 1,400 years old and still accurate about hawks, plums, and the behavior of thunder.
